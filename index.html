In short

I contact local businesses I believe I can genuinely help, using only publicly available business information. I never buy contact lists, never collect sensitive information, and will stop contacting you the moment you ask.

01About me

I help local businesses with websites, Google profiles, imagery, admin and simple automations. This notice explains what information I hold, why I hold it, how long I keep it and the rights you have over it.

02Information I hold

Before getting in touch, I may record:

  • the business name, address, phone number, email address and website
  • the owner's or manager's name, where it is publicly listed
  • publicly visible information about the business, such as its Google profile, reviews, website and social media pages
  • my own notes on where I believe I could help
  • any correspondence between us, including a request not to be contacted again

I do not collect special category (sensitive) information.

03Where it comes from

Publicly available sources only: Google Maps and Google Business Profiles, the business's own website and social media pages, Companies House and local directories. If we have met or corresponded, some information will also come directly from you.

04Why I use it and my lawful basis

I use this information to contact your business with a relevant offer of help and to keep a record of our correspondence.

My lawful basis is legitimate interests: I have a legitimate interest in offering my services to local businesses. I limit this to business contact information, make sure any contact is relevant to your business, and stop immediately if you ask. UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)

I only send unsolicited emails to limited companies and limited liability partnerships. Sole traders and other partnerships are contacted by letter or in person only.

05Tools and service providers

I store this information in my business email account and a private spreadsheet. I use AI tools to help research businesses and draft messages; every message is reviewed and sent by me personally, and no decisions about you are made by automated means. Some of these providers may process data outside the UK, in which case the safeguards required by UK data protection law apply.

07Your rights

You have the right to:

  • object to direct marketing: ask me to stop contacting you at any time, for any reason, and I will stop immediately
  • access the information I hold about you and receive a copy
  • have inaccurate information corrected
  • have your information deleted
  • restrict or object to how I use your information

To exercise any of these rights, email me or reply to any message from me. I will respond within one month.
